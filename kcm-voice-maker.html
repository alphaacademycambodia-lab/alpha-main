KCM Voice Maker

Makes the MP3 files for Khmer Conversational Mastery with Gemini text-to-speech, named so the lesson page plays them automatically. Your key stays in this browser and is sent only to Google.

Finished files are saved in this browser, so you can pause, close the tab and continue later. Lines already made are skipped. If Google says “quota” or “rate limit”, the tool waits and retries; on the free tier you may need to continue the next day.
