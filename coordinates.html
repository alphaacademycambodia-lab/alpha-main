Coordinates & Lines 📍

Draw any straight line y = mx + c, drag two points to find the gradient, midpoint and equation, then practise reading coordinates with a game of battleships.

      Find the four hidden ships

      Ships lie along the grid lines on points from −5 to 5: one of 4 points, one of 3 and two of 2. Type the coordinates — x first, then y — and fire.

      (,)

      Good to know

      Along the corridor, then up the stairs

      In (3, −2) the first number is across (x) and the second is up or down (y). Battleships only counts a shot if you get them the right way round.

      m is the slope, c is the start

      In y = mx + c, c is where the line crosses the y-axis and m is how far it climbs for each step to the right. A negative m goes downhill.

      Nothing leaves your device

      Everything runs in your browser and nothing is saved. The address bar keeps the line, so a link opens the same graph.
