Before you call

Quick answers

What happens in the free consultation?

We ask about the student's level, the subjects that are difficult, the timetable and the goal. Then we recommend a format, a session frequency and a tutor profile — and quote a rate. It usually takes about fifteen minutes, by phone or Telegram.

How soon can lessons start?

Most students have their first session within a week. Urgent exam preparation can often be arranged sooner.

What are the rates?

Rates depend on subject, level, format and frequency. Because plans are built per student, we quote after the consultation rather than publishing a one-size price list.

Can we start with a trial session?

Yes — many families begin with a single session to check the fit between student and tutor before committing to a weekly schedule.
